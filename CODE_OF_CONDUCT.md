# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Criticise code, designs and arguments as hard as
you like — that is what the repository is for — and leave people out of it.

Specifically, these are not welcome here: harassment of any kind, personal or
political attacks, demeaning or insulting comments, publishing anyone's private
information, and sexualised language or imagery.

Two more that matter in this particular repository:

**Do not use issues to promote a token.** Verdant creates markets and has no
opinion on the tokens launched through it. An issue that exists to get eyes on a
ticker will be closed.

**Do not disclose an unpatched vulnerability in public.** Not in an issue, not in
a pull request, not in a discussion. [Report it
privately](https://github.com/verdantfamily/verdant.family/security/advisories/new);
see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commits and
reviews — and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainers through [private
reporting](https://github.com/verdantfamily/verdant.family/security/advisories/new),
which is the confidential channel this repository has. Reports are handled
privately and the reporter's identity is not shared with the person reported.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that do not meet the standard
above, and may block accounts for repeated or severe behaviour. We will say what
was removed and why, unless saying so would expose the reporter.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
